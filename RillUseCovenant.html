01What Rill is, and what it is not

Rill records frozen batches, their quantities and the dates they went into the freezer, then helps you take stock oldest first. It is a personal bookkeeping tool for a household freezer.

Rill is not a safety service. It does not test, inspect or certify food. It does not compute shelf life, issue expiry warnings, or tell you whether a batch is still good. A stored date is an annotation you supplied, nothing more.

03Your records, your responsibility

Names, quantities, units, stored dates, locations and take-out history are entered by you. Keep them accurate enough for your own kitchen. If a figure is wrong, the app's own edit, delete and undo paths are how you correct it.

  • Undo reverses only the most recent take-out, and only while the affected stock has not changed since.
  • Deleting a batch removes its remaining quantity while its history stays in your log.
  • A reset clears local records on that device and cannot be reversed.
